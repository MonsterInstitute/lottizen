Hello Rafflebox team,

We run Lottizen (https://lottizen.com), a free, independent site about Canadian lotteries. Next to draw-game results and scratch-ticket data, it now covers charity lotteries: hospital home lotteries, team 50/50s and Catch the Ace. It tracks 91 of them so far, organized by province.

Many of the community raffles our readers ask about run on Rafflebox. Your Terms of Use say that automated access to the platform needs your express prior written consent. So before we include any Rafflebox raffle, we're writing to ask for that consent, on terms that suit you.

What we'd show for each raffle:
- Name, charity, licence number and licensing province
- Current jackpot, ticket packages, sales close and draw date
- The winning ticket number once you publish it

All of it is information that's already public on the raffle's own page. We never store or show winners' names.

What we'd send your raffles:
- **Visitors from the right province.** Each raffle gets a page on Lottizen's province page (for example https://lottizen.com/charity/ontario). The "Buy tickets" link goes straight to the raffle's page on Rafflebox, and only visitors whose province matches the licence see it. There's no affiliate or tracking parameter, and we take no payment per ticket. This follows AGCO's electronic raffle terms on seller payment and on marketing outside the licensing province.
- **Email subscribers.** Readers can follow a raffle with just an email address. We email them 3 days before a deadline, when a draw is close to selling out, and when the winning number is published. Each email links back to the raffle.
- **Search traffic.** Each raffle with published results gets a winning-numbers page, which is the search people run after a draw.

How we'd access the data:
- At whatever cadence and through whatever interface you prefer: the public event endpoints your raffle pages already use, a feed you provide, or only the raffles whose organizers opt in.
- We'd identify our requests with a named user agent and keep the volume well below anything that would load your service.
- Each listing would credit Rafflebox as the platform, if you'd like that.
- You or any charity can ask us to remove a listing at any time.

If you're open to this, a short written reply granting permission, with any conditions you want attached, is all we need. We're also happy to talk it through on a call.

Thank you for considering it.

The Lottizen Team
