The Western Canada Lottery Corporation covers the widest territory of any Canadian corporation — Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Alberta, and the three northern territories — but its claim process is refreshingly simple, hinging on a single dollar line. Here's how it works, whether you've won on [Western 6/49](/canada/western-6-49), [Western Max](/canada/western-max), or a national game bought out west.

## Where to claim, by prize size

| Prize amount | Where you claim |
| --- | --- |
| Up to $100 | Any Lotto Spot retailer (auto-validated; retailer must pay) |
| $100.01 – $1,000 | Retailers offering prize payment |
| $1,001 and up | Prize Payout office (by appointment) or by mail to WCLC |

That's the whole structure: **retailer up to $1,000, prize office or mail above it.** There's no separate online-claim tier as in BC or Quebec — larger prizes are handled in person or by mail.

WCLC's **Prize Payout offices** operate by appointment across the region: **St. Albert and Calgary** (Alberta), **Saskatoon and Regina** (Saskatchewan), **Winnipeg** (Manitoba), and **Yellowknife** (serving the territories). The head office is at **242 Hargrave Street, Suite 300, Winnipeg**. There's no dedicated Yukon office — Yukon claims route through mail or Player Care.

One practical note: WCLC deliberately doesn't publish a ticket-mailing address. **Call Player Care at 1-800-665-3313 before mailing** any claim of $1,000 or more, and they'll give you mailing instructions. If you use the Lotto Spot app, present the barcode — printed copies of a digital ticket aren't accepted.

## Check your ticket before you claim

Sign — or legibly print your legal name on — the ticket as soon as you can after buying it; WCLC notes that "retailers cannot check tickets without a name." Then verify the win through an official channel: the Lotto Spot app's barcode scanner, an in-store ticket-checker, the posted results, or a regional results hotline. Don't rely on eyeballing the numbers, and don't run a big-win ticket through a scanner repeatedly — confirm the amount once, then follow the claim route for that prize size.

## The one-year deadline

Lottery tickets — Lotto Max, Lotto 6/49, Daily Grand, the Western games, Poker Lotto, Extra, Keno, and the Pick games — **expire one year from the draw date**. Sport Select is one year from the last game played, and scratch tickets follow the expiry printed on the back. As WCLC puts it, "be sure to claim the prize within a year of the draw date."

## What to bring

WCLC's documentation is a little more formal than some corporations:

- **Your signed original ticket** — print or sign your legal name on it as soon as you can after purchase (retailers can't even check an unsigned ticket).
- **Government ID** — one piece for prizes under $10,000, **two pieces for $10,000 or more**.
- **A signed Claimant Information Sheet.**
- **A Statutory Declaration** sworn before a Qualified Witness — and prize-office staff can act as that witness, so you don't need to arrange it separately.

Notably, WCLC does **not** ask for your SIN on its claim forms.

The **Statutory Declaration** is the step that surprises first-time claimants. It's a formal legal statement, sworn before a Qualified Witness, that you are the rightful owner of the ticket — the same kind of sworn declaration you'd make before a notary. The convenience is that **prize-office staff can act as the witness**, so a claim office visit handles the ticket, the ID check, and the declaration in one appointment. For a mailed claim, ask Player Care how to have the declaration witnessed before you send anything.

## How you'll be paid

WCLC pays by **cheque**, and with a specific limit worth knowing: "only one prize cheque can be issued — to a maximum of two people." (For a larger group, that's what the trustee process below is for.) The prize is a single, tax-free lump sum — WCLC states flatly that "all cash and merchandise prizes paid by WCLC are tax-free within Canada."

The one exception is a "for life" game. [Daily Grand](/canada/daily-grand)'s top prize can be taken as **$1,000 a day for life** (guaranteed at least 20 years) **or a single $7-million payment** — and if you elect the annuity stream, that choice can carry tax implications. For everything else, the number you won is the number you get. See [Are Lottery Winnings Taxable in Canada?](/guides/are-lottery-winnings-taxable-in-canada) for the full picture.

## Claiming as a group

For a pooled ticket, call Player Care first. The **trustee** prints their name followed by "**In Trust**" on the back of the ticket to mark it as group-owned; WCLC then pays the **full prize to the trustee**, who distributes each member's share. A free **Group Buying Agreement** records everyone's share — it doesn't need to be filed with WCLC, but WCLC will ask for it on a group win over $1,000, and the Statutory Declaration lists all sharers. If you play in a pool, read [Group Play and Office Pools](/guides/lottery-group-play-office-pools-canada) before you win, not after.

## Publicity: no anonymity

WCLC is blunt: it "has no obligation to pay a prize unless the winner gives WCLC the right to publish the winner's name, city or town of residence and recent photograph." A **winner interview and photograph are required for all claims of $10,000 or more.** Unlike OLG and BCLC, WCLC did **not** adopt the 2026 move to first-name-and-initial news releases — its publicity rules are unchanged. If privacy is a concern, read [Can You Stay Anonymous After Winning?](/guides/lottery-winner-anonymity-canada); the safety-based exception exists but is rare.

## The short version

Under $1,000, any retailer; over $1,000, a prize office by appointment or a mailed claim (call Player Care first). Bring your signed ticket, ID (two pieces above $10,000), and be ready to swear a statutory declaration a prize-office witness can administer. The prize is tax-free and paid by cheque — with your name and photo public above $10,000. For the bigger picture on a large win, start with [What to Do If You Win the Lottery in Canada](/guides/what-to-do-if-you-win-the-lottery-in-canada).
